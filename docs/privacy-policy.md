# Wean Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 10, 2026

## Introduction

Wean ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the Wean mobile application (the "App").

**Key Principle:** We prioritize your privacy. Wean uses anonymous authentication and minimal data collection to ensure your substance use tracking remains private and secure.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide

**Dose Tracking Data**
- Dose amounts (grams/ounces)
- Timestamps of doses
- Substance types
- Personal notes (if you add them)
- Goal settings and taper plans

**Anonymous Authentication**
- We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which generates a random user ID
- No email address, phone number, or personal information is required
- Your identity cannot be traced back to you through this ID

### Automatically Collected Data

**Usage Analytics** (if you opt in)
- App feature usage (which screens you visit, how often)
- Performance data (app crashes, loading times)
- Device information (device type, OS version, app version)

**Note:** All analytics are anonymized and cannot be linked to your identity.

### Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:
- Your name, email, or phone number (unless you contact support)
- Location data
- Contacts or photos
- Social media profiles
- Payment information (handled by RevenueCat/payment processors)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

1. **Provide Core Features**
   - Store your dose tracking data
   - Generate taper plans and progress visualizations
   - Send reminders (if enabled)

2. **Improve the App**
   - Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
   - Fix bugs and improve performance
   - Develop new features based on user needs

3. **Premium Subscriptions**
   - Process payments through RevenueCat
   - Manage subscription status
   - Provide premium features to paying users

## Data Storage and Security

**Where Your Data is Stored**
- All user data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
- Servers are located in the United States
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest

**Data Retention**
- Your dose data is retained as long as you use the App
- If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days
- Anonymous user IDs cannot be recovered after deletion

**Security Measures**
- Firebase Authentication with secure token-based access
- Firestore Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access
- Regular security audits and updates
- No third-party access to your personal dose data

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control Your Data
- View all your dose data within the App
- Export your data (Premium feature)
- Delete individual doses or your entire account

### Data Deletion
To delete your account and all associated data:
1. Open the App
2. Go to Settings → Account
3. Tap "Delete Account"
4. Confirm deletion

All data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

### Opt-Out of Analytics
You can disable usage analytics in Settings → Privacy.

### Communication Preferences
- We do not send marketing emails (you're anonymous!)
- In-app notifications can be disabled in Settings
- Push notifications are opt-in only

## Children's Privacy

Wean is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:
- Displaying a notice in the App
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect and how we use it
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)

To exercise these rights, delete your account through the App or contact us at privacy@wean.app.

## International Users (GDPR Compliance)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

**Legal Basis for Processing**
- Consent (you agree to this policy when using the App)
- Legitimate interests (improving the App and providing support)

**Your GDPR Rights**
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification (correct inaccurate data)
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability (export your data)
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@wean.app.

**Data Transfers**
Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We ensure adequate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

## Do Not Sell My Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

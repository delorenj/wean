## Full Description
(4000 character limit)

**Take Control of Your Kratom Use with Wean**

Wean helps you reduce or quit kratom through structured, gradual tapering. No judgment, just progress.

**Why Wean Works**

• **Accurate Tracking**: Log every dose with timestamps. Know exactly how much you're taking.
• **Visual Progress**: See your daily totals and weekly trends at a glance with beautiful charts.
• **Smart Taper Plans**: Get personalized reduction schedules based on your current intake.
• **Progress Celebrations**: Hit milestones and earn badges as you reduce your use.
• **Privacy First**: Anonymous authentication means your data stays private.

**For Habitual Users**

If you're taking kratom daily and want to reduce or quit, cold turkey isn't the answer. Wean makes tapering manageable by breaking it down into small, achievable steps. Our taper algorithm accounts for kratom's long half-life, helping you avoid harsh withdrawal symptoms while making steady progress.

**Track Everything**

• Quick dose entry with slider interface
• Gram and ounce support
• Daily, weekly, and monthly views
• Historical calendar to identify patterns
• Reminder system for scheduled doses

**Visualize Your Journey**

• Daily dose gauge shows progress vs. target
• Timeline view of all doses
• Trend analysis over time
• Goal tracking and celebrations

**Smart Tapering**

• Evidence-based 5-10% weekly reduction
• Customizable taper schedules
• Milestone tracking
• Flexible goals (quit entirely or reduce to weekend use)

**Premium Features**

Upgrade to Wean Pro for:
• Advanced analytics and insights
• Unlimited dose history
• Custom taper plans
• Export your data
• Ad-free experience
• Priority support

**Safe & Private**

Your data is yours. Wean uses anonymous authentication—no email, no phone number, no personal info required. All data is encrypted and stored securely with Firebase.

**Made by Someone Who Gets It**

Wean was created by someone who's been there. We understand the challenge of reducing kratom use, and we've built the tool we wish existed when we needed it most.

**Start Your Journey Today**

Whether you want to quit completely or just cut back, Wean gives you the structure and accountability to make it happen. Download now and take the first step toward taking control.

---

**Note:** Wean is a harm reduction tool, not a medical treatment. Consult a healthcare provider for medical advice about substance use.
